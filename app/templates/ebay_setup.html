Create an eBay developer account and keyset

  1. Sign up (free) at developer.ebay.com — use or link the eBay account you sell with. Approval can take a day.
  2. Open Application Keys, give the app a name (e.g. “FlipDesk”), and create a Production keyset (or Sandbox to experiment).
  3. eBay won't activate a Production keyset until you complete step 3 (account-deletion notifications) — do steps 2–3 next.

Give this app a public HTTPS address

eBay redirects back to this app after you sign in, and sends account-deletion notices to it, so it needs an HTTPS URL that eBay can reach. Typical options: your existing reverse proxy (Nginx Proxy Manager, Traefik, Caddy, SWAG) or a Cloudflare Tunnel. Only /ebay/* and /privacy need to be reachable from the internet if you want to keep the rest on your LAN.

No public URL? You can still connect: eBay will send your browser to the accepted URL, and you paste that address into step 6. You'd need to request an exemption from account-deletion notifications from eBay instead of step 3.
